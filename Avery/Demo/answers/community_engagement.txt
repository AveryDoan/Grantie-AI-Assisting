Most of my free time goes into community projects. Since 2022 I have run Code Pahadi, a free weekend coding club for girls in rural schools. It has grown from 9 students in one classroom to more than 180 girls in 6 schools, with 14 volunteer mentors, most of them my former students. In 2025 I gave my weekends to the Gorkha flood recovery project, where I led 6 volunteers to train 300 villagers in digital skills. We helped 41 small businesses move their orders online. I also mentor younger students whenever I can find the time. In Darwin I plan to volunteer with local community organisations and mentor other international students.
